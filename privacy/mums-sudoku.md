# Privacy Policy — Mum's Sudoku

_Last updated: 2026-04-22_

Mum's Sudoku is a single-player puzzle game. We respect your privacy and
collect as little data as possible.

## What we store on your device

- **Game progress**: the puzzle you're currently solving, your move
  history, and your settings (language, dark mode, sound, etc.).
- **Statistics**: a record of every puzzle you complete (difficulty,
  variant, time, mistake count, hint count). Used to populate the
  Statistics page on your device.
- **Achievements**: which achievements you've earned and lifetime
  counters (correct-streak, mistakes, hints).

All of the above lives on your device only.

## What we send to Apple Game Center / Google Play Games

If you sign in to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games
(Android), the game submits:

- Your **completion time** for the daily puzzle, to the
  `lb_daily_fastest` leaderboard.
- Your **total puzzles solved**, to the `lb_total_solved` leaderboard.
- **Achievement unlocks**, mirrored to the platform's achievement
  system.

This data is governed by Apple's or Google's respective privacy
policies. We do not see, store, or process it on our own servers.

## What we do NOT do

- No advertising, no ad SDKs, no behavioural tracking.
- No analytics SDK. We do not record what you tap, where you tap, or
  how often you open the app.
- No account system of our own. No email address, no password.
- No network requests other than Game Center / Play Games (when
  signed in).
- No personal data is sold or shared with third parties, ever.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any
personal information from children.
