# Privacy Policy — Mum's Ludo

_Last updated: 2026-04-24_

Mum's Ludo is an offline board game for up to four local players (any
mix of humans and CPU). We respect your privacy and collect as little
data as possible.

## What we store on your device

- **Match progress**: the match currently in play (tokens, whose turn,
  dice state) and your settings (language, dark mode, sound, haptics,
  rule toggles, per-seat Human/CPU configuration).
- **Statistics**: a record of every match you complete (winner seat,
  seat Human/CPU mix, elapsed time, roll count). Used to populate the
  Statistics page on your device.

All of the above lives on your device only.

## What we send to Apple Game Center / Google Play Games

Nothing, at present. Leaderboards and achievements are not wired up in
this release. If we add them in future, a signed-in account would only
submit aggregate match stats (time, match count) — never any personal
information.

## What we do NOT do

- No advertising, no ad SDKs, no behavioural tracking.
- No analytics SDK. We do not record what you tap, where you tap, or
  how often you open the app.
- No account system of our own. No email address, no password.
- No network requests. The game is fully offline.
- No personal data is sold or shared with third parties, ever.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any
personal information from children.
